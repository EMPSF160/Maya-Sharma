Creative Journey

From Rough Sketch to Final Book Spread

Every picture book is crafted with emotional authenticity, storytelling rhythm, and custom textured digital brushes on iPad Pro in Procreate.

1

Thumbnails & Character Rhythm

Reading the manuscript to identify emotional turns. Exploring spontaneous character gestures, eye-lines, page-turn pacing, and compositional focal points on digital canvas.

2

Color Scripting & Textures

Establishing a cohesive palette that evokes childhood nostalgia. Layering hand-crafted digital gouache brushes, colored pencil tooth, and luminous Indian jewel tones.

3

Print-Ready Publisher Delivery

Meticulously prepared CMYK 300 DPI spreads with bleed margins, calibrated for offset printing. Collaborating closely with art directors for typography harmony.

About Maya

Children's Book Author & Illustrator • India

Specializing in picture books, chapter books, book covers & character design.

Hello! I am Maya, a children's book author and illustrator based in India. Art and storytelling have always been my natural language for expressing the wondrous, colorful, and tender moments of childhood.

My work is deeply rooted in warmth, emotional authenticity, and everyday joys. Whether depicting a joyful Indian family festival, rainy day adventures, or quiet starlit bedrooms, I strive to create pictures that make young readers feel safe, delighted, and curious about the world around them.

Tools & Medium: I work primarily in digital using an iPad Pro and Procreate, carefully crafting custom textured brushes that simulate traditional gouache, watercolors, and colored pencil grain.
